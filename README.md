## The entry

> Grandpa turned the radio down and said, 'listen, the crickets are better,' and I rolled my eyes then, but now I turn my own radio down at dusk because he was right — the crickets were always better.

One sentence, under 280 characters. Two characters, one spoken line,
a reversal (the eye-roll becomes the inherited habit), and a closing
image that reframes the opening: the radio was turned down for the
crickets, and the crickets were always better.
